HTML Special Characters

Entity: a mechanism for writing special characters or symbols in HTML, such as mathematical symbols, characters in most languages and many other symbols. Many HTML entities can be specified by name, by a decimal number, or by a hexadecimal number.

Example: a copyright logo.
© - &copy;
© - &#169;
© - &#x000A9;

Emojis

Emoji characters can be displayed in a webpage with a decimal or hexadecimal entity. Example: the happy face: 😀 or 😀

Entities for reserved characters

The < and & characters can be displayed using entities. &lt; for less than, and & amp; for the ampersand. EX. 2 < 3 = True

Non- Breaking characters

A non-breaking character is an inter-word character that per,its treating the words on both sides to be one word

Non-breaking hyphen

A non breaking hyphen, ‑, looks like a regular hyphen but acts like a normal character in the middle of a word. Non‑breaking hyphen.

Non-breaking space

a Non breaking space  , looks like a single space but acts like a normal character in the middle of a word.
